About QueueLess

Organized, transparent college services

QueueLess replaces crowded lines with a digital queue. Students pick a service, receive a token and follow their position live, while staff call tokens in order from an admin dashboard.

  • Fair first-come, first-served ordering
  • Waiting time based on real service records
  • Clear status for every token and counter
